# Visuels AtelierConnect

Ces photographies d'illustration ont été créées avec l'outil intégré de génération
d'images (compétence `imagegen`). Elles représentent l'ambiance créative du projet,
pas des photographies documentaires des centres enregistrés en base.

Les fichiers JPEG sont servis localement par Angular. Les originaux PNG générés
sont conservés hors du dépôt ; une conversion JPEG à qualité 85 réduit le poids
sans recadrage ni modification du contenu de l'image.

| Fichier | Usage |
| --- | --- |
| `atelier-hero.jpg` | Accueil, connexion participant et cartes |
| `atelier-watercolor.jpg` | Collection, création d'atelier et cartes |
| `atelier-studio.jpg` | Connexion administrateur, tableaux de bord et en-têtes |

## Prompts finaux

**Accueil.** Premium editorial lifestyle photograph for AtelierConnect, a Belgian
community creative workshop booking website. A luminous contemporary pottery and
art studio, four adult friends of diverse ages and backgrounds collaborating
around a natural oak table, shaping handmade clay bowls, linen aprons, sage green
cabinetry, warm cream plaster walls, terracotta vessels, houseplants and arched
windows with late-afternoon sunlight. Authentic candid interaction, lifelike
anatomy and hands, tactile natural materials. Wide landscape, people and table
center-right, muted forest green, ivory and burnt orange. No text, logos,
watermarks or UI.

**Aquarelle.** Close-up of two adults' hands painting botanical watercolors
together at an oak worktable. Watercolor paints in ceramic dishes, brushes,
sage and terracotta pigments, textured ivory paper, soft natural daylight,
plants. Premium craft magazine photography, realistic hands and materials,
restrained warm palette, shallow depth of field. Wide landscape, no text,
logos, watermarks or UI.

**Studio.** Sunlit contemporary creative studio ready for a small group of six
adults. Large oak table with linen aprons, ceramic bowls, paper and brushes,
forest green cabinets, ivory limewashed walls, tall arched windows, terracotta
pottery and houseplants. Welcoming Belgian design, quiet morning sunshine,
tactile natural materials, interior design magazine quality. No people.
Wide landscape, no writing, signs, logos, watermarks or UI.
